# <span class="text-4xl font-bold">Privacy Policy for Stuns</span>

<div class="bg-blue-50 dark:bg-zinc-800 p-4 rounded-lg mb-8 border border-blue-200 dark:border-zinc-700">

**Effective date:** May 9, 2026

Stuns respects your privacy. Stuns reads Apple Health data only with your permission to provide fitness and wellness insights inside the app.

</div>

## <span class="text-2xl font-semibold">What Stuns Accesses</span>

With your consent, Stuns may read:

- Steps
- Active calories
- Exercise minutes
- Sleep data (including stages, when available)
- Heart rate
- Workouts (including route/GPS data, when available)

You control which data types Stuns can access in iOS Health permissions.

## <span class="text-2xl font-semibold">How Data Is Used</span>

Your data is used only to power app features, including:

- Daily dashboard metrics
- Sleep details and charts
- Workout details and maps
- Monthly summaries and trends

## <span class="text-2xl font-semibold">Data Sharing</span>

- ✓ Health data is processed on-device.
- ✓ Stuns does not sell your data.
- ✓ Stuns does not use Health data for advertising or marketing.
- ✓ Stuns does not share Health data with data brokers.

## <span class="text-2xl font-semibold">Data Storage</span>

Stuns stores only what is needed for app functionality on your device.  
If cloud/account features are added in future, this policy will be updated.

## <span class="text-2xl font-semibold">Your Choices</span>

You can revoke Health access anytime in:  
**Settings > Privacy & Security > Health > Stuns**

You can also delete the app to remove its local data.

## <span class="text-2xl font-semibold">Third-Party Services</span>

Stuns uses Apple system frameworks (such as HealthKit and MapKit).  
Any future third-party services affecting privacy will be disclosed here.
